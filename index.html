04 — About

Matt
Kirkham

Matt Kirkham is a Cambridge-based photographer, videographer, and filmmaker available for commercial and creative work.

His work spans theatre, events, brand and commercial video, and independent film — taking projects from shoot through to final grade. A BFI Film Academy alumnus, he is a committee member of Silver Street Productions, a production company at Queens' College, Cambridge.

Based in Cambridge and available for commissions across the UK. Get in touch to discuss your project.
