04 — About

Matt
Kirkham

Matt Kirkham is a Cambridge-based photographer, videographer, and filmmaker available for commercial and creative work.

Whether working with a brand, an organisation, or an independent production, his approach is the same: to understand what story needs telling and build the visual language to tell it. Taking projects from initial consultation through to final grade, he works as a single point of contact across photography, video, and film.

His work spans theatre, events and portraiture photography, brand and commercial video, and independent film. A BFI Film Academy alumnus, he is a committee member of Silver Street Productions, a production company at Queens' College, Cambridge.

Based in Cambridge and available for commissions across the UK. Get in touch to discuss your project.
