04 — About

Matt
Kirkham

Matt Kirkham is a Cambridge-based photographer, videographer, and filmmaker, available for commercial and creative work.

Taking projects from pre-production through to final grade, his aim is always the same: to understand what a project needs to say, and build the visual language to say it. His work spans theatre and events photography, portraiture, brand and commercial video, and narrative short film.

A BFI Film Academy alumnus, he is a committee member of Silver Street Productions, a production company at Queens' College, Cambridge. Based in Cambridge and available for commissions across the UK. Get in touch to discuss your project.
